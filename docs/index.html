GOOD TO KNOW

Before the encore.

Can I use Hive offline?+

Local playback works offline after the required app and library activation is complete and your resources are available. Downloads and online activation need internet access. SmartLink needs a local network, which is separate from internet access.

Are songs included?+

Use your own compatible songs and media. Some protected libraries need their own activation; app activation does not unlock every library.

Do I need the SmartLink app to control Hive?+

No. Enable the player’s remote server and open its local address in a browser on the same network. Enter the PIN if one is configured. The SmartLink app adds wireless microphone streaming.

Does the score measure my singing?+

Scores are for entertainment. They do not measure microphone input or vocal accuracy.

How do I update?+

Get a newer official release for your platform. On Android, install an update signed with the same release key to keep your app data. Keep your resources and recordings backed up.
